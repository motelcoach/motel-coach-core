**What Are Google Hotel Ads?**

Google Hotel Ads are a powerful [accommodation marketing tool](/motel-marketing/). It allows you to display your motel or hotel's live rates, availability, photos, and a booking link directly on Google Search, Maps, and Google Travel. When someone searches for a motel in your area, they’ll see your property in a price comparison section alongside major online travel agents (OTAs) like Booking.com or Expedia.

Example: A potential guest searches “motels in Mackay” — your motel can appear with price, star rating, and a “Book Now” link under your official name.

**How It Works:**

Google Hotel Ads use a data feed from your Property Management System (PMS) or channel manager to sync your:

*   Rates
    
*   Room availability
    
*   Booking links
    
*   You’ll need to partner with a Google-approved integration partner (like SiteMinder, Cloudbeds, or Little Hotelier) to get started. Here is a link to[Google's Hotel Ad Connectivity Partners](https://developers.google.com/hotels/connectivity-partners).
    

**Bidding Models (Flexible Payment Options):**

*   Cost-Per-Click (CPC): You pay each time someone clicks your ad.
    
*   Commission Per Stay (CPA): You pay a % only when someone books and stays.
    
*   Pay-Per-Conversion: You pay a % only if someone completes a booking.
    

**Factors to consider**

*   Even when you pay for ads through Google Hotel Ads, potential guests will still heavily lean on your Google Rating. A higher Google Rating will give potential guests confidence to book accommodation at your motel or hotel.

*   Google is all about providing information to searches to help them learn or make decisions. Google will show the potential guest all of the booking options available for your property and the rates on each of these channels. If your rate is not the lowest or equivalent to other options your direct bookings will drop significantly.

*   Your visibility on Google Hotel Ads is subject to how much you pay.

**Key Benefits for Motel Owners:**

*   More Direct Bookings – Show your official website with live prices.

*   Reduced OTA Fees – Compete directly with Booking.com and Expedia.

*   Increased Visibility – Appear in front of travelers ready to book.

*   Professional Trust – Your Google listing looks more complete and authoritative.

Is It Worth It for Small Motels?

Yes. Even for small properties, Google Hotel Ads can level the playing field and give you more control over how guests find and book with you. Combined with [Motel SEO](/motel-seo/) it can be a really powerful tool to drive direct bookings and profitability. It's especially useful if you have a strong local presence and review score.

**Next Steps to Get Started:**

*   Make sure your website supports online bookings.
    
*   Use a booking engine or channel manager that integrates with Google.
    
*   Choose a bidding model that fits your budget.
    
*   Track performance (clicks, bookings, revenue) to optimize your listing.
    
Tip: Keep your Google Business Profile updated with fresh photos, correct amenities, and positive reviews. Having a Google Business Profile that has appealing photos and quality guest review score will help to convert more visitors to paying guests through Google Hotel Ads. It also boosts your organic ranking on Google. A strategy called [SEO](/motel-seo/) or Search Engine Optimisation.
